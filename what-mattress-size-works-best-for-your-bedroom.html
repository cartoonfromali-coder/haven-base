Mattress Buying Guide

What Mattress Size Works Best for Your Bedroom?

The right mattress size needs to balance sleeping space with the practical dimensions of your bedroom. Here is how to make that decision before buying.

Quick answer The best mattress size is the largest option that comfortably suits the sleepers while leaving enough usable space for walking, furniture and everyday bedroom access.

Start With the Bedroom

It is easy to choose a mattress based only on how much sleeping space you would like. But the bedroom needs to accommodate the complete bed as well as the people using it.

Measure the available floor area first. Then consider where the bed can realistically be positioned without blocking furniture or access routes.

Think About the Main Size Categories

Single

A practical choice where floor space is limited or the room is intended for one sleeper.

Small double

Can provide more sleeping width while taking less room than a standard double.

Double

A common option for couples, provided the bedroom can comfortably accommodate the complete bed.

Measure the Mattress Area Carefully

Measure both the width and length of the space where the mattress and bed will sit. Remember that the external dimensions of the finished bed can be larger than the mattress itself.

This is especially important with upholstered or storage-focused bed frames, where the frame may extend beyond the mattress footprint.

Measurement tip: Check the manufacturer's stated dimensions for both the mattress and the complete bed. Use the larger external footprint when planning your bedroom layout.

Consider Who Will Use the Mattress

A mattress size should also reflect the number of sleepers and how much personal space they need at night.

For one person, a smaller size may leave more room for wardrobes, desks or other bedroom furniture. Couples may prefer a wider mattress if the room can accommodate it comfortably.

Think About Future Use

A mattress can last for years, so think beyond your immediate bedroom arrangement. If the room may later serve a different purpose, consider whether the chosen size will remain practical.

However, future possibilities should not outweigh your current practical requirements. A mattress that makes the room difficult to use every day may not be worth choosing simply because it is larger.

Check Furniture Before Deciding

Drawers, wardrobes, bedside tables and desks all compete for floor space. Measure their positions and check that doors and drawers can still open after the bed is installed.

It can help to sketch the room on paper or mark the proposed bed footprint on the floor before buying.

Do Not Forget Access Around the Mattress

Sleeping space is only one consideration. You also need room to get into bed, change the bedding and move around the bedroom.

A slightly smaller mattress that leaves a clear, practical route can sometimes work better than a larger mattress that leaves the room feeling obstructed.

Mattress Size Checklist

  • Measure the available bedroom floor space.
  • Choose a size suitable for the number of sleepers.
  • Check the complete bed's external dimensions.
  • Consider wardrobes, drawers and bedside furniture.
  • Leave practical walking and access routes.
  • Think about how the room may be used in the future.
  • Compare the mattress dimensions with the bed frame specifications.
