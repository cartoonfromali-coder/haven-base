Ottoman Beds Guide

What Size Ottoman Storage Do You Actually Need?

Choosing an ottoman bed is not only about having as much storage as possible. The right setup depends on what you need to keep and how your bedroom works.

Quick answer Choose ottoman storage according to the items you need to store, the available bed size and how frequently you need access. More capacity is useful only when it remains practical to organise and reach.

Start With Your Storage Needs

Before comparing ottoman beds, make a simple list of the things you would realistically store underneath the mattress. This gives you a much better idea of the capacity you need.

Common examples include spare duvets, pillows, blankets, seasonal clothing and other bedroom textiles. Items that are used occasionally are especially suitable for under-bed storage.

Think in Terms of Storage Levels

Occasional storage

Suitable when you mainly want somewhere for spare bedding or seasonal items.

Regular storage

Useful when the bedroom has limited cupboards and the bed will perform an important storage role.

Maximum capacity

Worth considering when the bedroom has very little alternative storage and the available space under the bed can be fully used.

The Bed Size Affects Storage Potential

The available storage area is naturally influenced by the overall size of the bed. A larger bed can provide a larger footprint, while a smaller bed may have more limited internal space.

However, do not choose a larger bed purely to gain storage. The mattress size should first suit the sleeper and the bedroom. Storage should be treated as an additional benefit rather than the only reason to change bed size.

Check the Bedroom Before Choosing

The internal capacity of an ottoman bed is only part of the decision. You also need enough room around the bed to use it comfortably.

Measure bedside tables, wardrobes and other furniture. Consider doors and walking routes as well. The bed should not make the room difficult to use simply because it offers more storage.

Practical tip: Measure the space where the bed will sit before comparing storage configurations. This helps you understand whether the complete bed will work in the room, not just whether the mattress will fit.

What Should You Store?

Ottoman storage is particularly useful for bulky bedroom items that take up valuable cupboard space. Spare bedding is an obvious example because it can occupy a significant amount of wardrobe or linen cupboard space.

Seasonal items can also work well because they do not need to be accessed every day. Keeping these items together can make the rest of the bedroom easier to organise.

Do You Really Need Maximum Storage?

Not necessarily. If you already have plenty of wardrobe and cupboard space, a smaller amount of storage may be perfectly adequate.

On the other hand, if your bedroom has limited built-in storage, an ottoman bed can help reduce the need for additional furniture. The important thing is to match the storage capacity with your actual requirements.

Storage Checklist

  • List the items you want to store.
  • Separate everyday items from seasonal belongings.
  • Consider the bed size you actually need.
  • Measure the available bedroom floor space.
  • Check surrounding furniture and access routes.
  • Choose storage capacity based on realistic use.
  • Make sure the complete bed remains practical for the room.
