Privacy Policy

A clear explanation of the information that may be collected while you explore our bedroom guides, recommendations and website pages.

Our Approach to Privacy

Haven & Base is designed primarily as an information and product discovery website. Visitors can explore articles and recommendations without needing to provide personal information for ordinary browsing.

This policy explains the circumstances in which information may be received through the website and how that information may be handled.

Information Created During a Visit

Normal web requests can produce technical information such as IP address, browser details, operating system, device characteristics, referring source and pages visited.

This type of information can help us understand technical problems, security issues and general website usage.

Information You Voluntarily Provide

If you use a contact feature provided on the website, we may receive the information you decide to include. This can include your name, contact details and the contents of your enquiry.

There is no need to include payment information, passwords or other confidential details when contacting us.

How Website Information Helps Us

  • Maintain reliable website operation.
  • Improve the quality and organisation of our content.
  • Understand broad visitor behaviour.
  • Respond to legitimate enquiries.
  • Identify technical faults or suspicious activity.
  • Evaluate referral and affiliate links.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies or similar browser technologies may be used for functionality, preferences, analytics and measurement.

Some technologies may belong to third-party providers. You can normally adjust cookie controls through your browser, although restricting them may affect certain features.

Affiliate Referrals

Certain product links may be affiliate links. When a visitor follows such a link, the external retailer or affiliate network may use referral tracking to record the source of the visit.

Any information collected after leaving Haven & Base is governed by the privacy policy of the external website. We do not operate the external retailer's checkout, payment or delivery systems.

Third-Party Services

Hosting providers, analytics services, security providers and other technical partners may process limited information where required to provide their services.

External retailers and websites linked from our pages have their own data practices. We recommend reviewing their policies before sharing information with them.

Protection and Retention

Appropriate measures may be used to reduce risks associated with information handled through the website. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was obtained and according to the retention policies of relevant third-party services.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, restriction or other use of your personal information.

You can raise an applicable privacy request using the contact method available on this website.

Children's Information

We do not intentionally request personal information from children through this website. If information has been submitted unnecessarily, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

Changes to Our Privacy Notice

This policy can be updated when our website, technologies, services or applicable requirements change. The revised version will be published on this page.

Contacting Us

Privacy questions and requests may be submitted through the contact method provided on the website.
