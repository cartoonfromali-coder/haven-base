How to Organise the Storage Inside an Ottoman Bed

A well-organised storage area makes an ottoman bed much more useful. These simple ideas can help you make better use of the space underneath.

Quick answer Group similar items together, keep frequently used belongings accessible and reserve harder-to-reach areas for seasonal storage. Avoid filling the space so tightly that lifting and removing items becomes difficult.

Start by Sorting What You Own

Before placing everything inside the bed, take a few minutes to sort your belongings. This prevents the storage area from becoming a second version of an overcrowded cupboard.

Separate bedding, seasonal clothing and other bedroom items into groups. You can then decide which things deserve the easiest access.

Create Simple Storage Zones

Everyday bedding

Keep spare sheets, pillowcases or regularly used blankets where they can be reached easily.

Seasonal items

Use less accessible areas for winter duvets, heavier blankets or seasonal clothing.

Occasional items

Reserve space for belongings that are useful but rarely needed throughout the year.

Keep Frequently Used Items Accessible

The most convenient storage arrangement is not necessarily the one that holds the most. If you need to move several layers of belongings every time you want a spare duvet, the storage quickly becomes frustrating.

Put frequently used items in the easiest area to reach and move seasonal belongings further away.

Organisation tip: Think about frequency rather than simply size. The things you use most should generally be the easiest to reach.

Use Soft Storage Where Appropriate

Fabric storage bags or similar organisers can help keep smaller textiles grouped together. They can also make seasonal items easier to identify when you next open the bed.

Avoid packing the storage area so tightly that items become difficult to remove. The goal is accessible storage rather than maximum compression.

Store Bulky Bedding Carefully

Duvets and blankets can occupy a surprising amount of cupboard space. An ottoman bed can provide a useful place for these larger items, especially when they are not needed every day.

Fold or loosely compress items according to their material and keep them grouped by type. This makes it easier to find what you need without disturbing everything else.

Keep a Clear Idea of What Is Inside

If you use the bed for seasonal storage, it can be easy to forget exactly what has been placed underneath. A simple written list or labelled storage bag can make future access much easier.

This is especially helpful when the storage area contains several categories of belongings that are only used occasionally.

Do Not Overfill the Storage Area

It can be tempting to use every available centimetre, but overfilling can make the storage difficult to manage. Leave enough room to lift and remove items without having to empty the entire area first.

Always follow the manufacturer's guidance for using and operating the ottoman mechanism.

Simple Organisation Checklist

  • Sort belongings into clear categories.
  • Keep frequently used items easy to reach.
  • Use less accessible areas for seasonal belongings.
  • Group smaller textiles in suitable storage bags.
  • Keep bulky bedding folded and organised.
  • Label seasonal storage where useful.
  • Avoid overfilling the storage compartment.
  • Follow the bed manufacturer's storage and lifting guidance.
