Should an Ottoman Bed Have a Full or Partial Lift?

The way an ottoman bed opens affects how you use its storage. Here is what to consider when comparing different lift arrangements.

Quick answer A full lift can make a larger storage area easier to access, while a partial or differently arranged opening may work better where room layout or access is restricted. The best choice depends on the bedroom and how you intend to use the storage.

Why the Lift Design Matters

Ottoman storage is useful because the space beneath the mattress can hold bulky bedroom items without taking up additional floor area. But storage capacity is only part of the experience.

The opening mechanism determines how easily you can reach the contents. This makes the lift design particularly important when the bed is positioned close to wardrobes, walls or other furniture.

Full Lift Access

Wide access

A full lifting arrangement can give you a broad view of the storage area, making it easier to organise larger items.

Useful for bulky items

Large bedding and seasonal textiles can be easier to place and remove when more of the storage area is accessible.

Partial or More Limited Access

Layout considerations

A different opening arrangement may be more practical where surrounding furniture limits how the bed can be opened.

Specific storage habits

If you mainly store a smaller group of frequently used items, you may not need the widest possible access.

Look at the Space Around the Bed

The bedroom layout should influence your decision. Measure the area around the bed and consider what happens when the storage is opened.

Pay attention to wardrobes, bedside tables, shelving, windows and low furniture. The opening action needs to be practical within the actual room, not just in an empty showroom-style space.

Layout tip: Think about the position of the bed and the furniture around it before choosing an opening arrangement. The most useful storage is storage you can access without rearranging the bedroom.

Think About What You Will Store

If you plan to store bulky duvets, blankets or large seasonal items, wider access can make loading and removing them easier.

If the storage is mainly for smaller items or things you access less frequently, the access arrangement may be less important than the overall bed dimensions and room layout.

How Often Will You Open the Bed?

Your storage habits also matter. A bed used for weekly bedding changes needs convenient access, while a storage area containing winter clothing may only be opened a few times a year.

Think about the tasks you will actually perform rather than simply choosing the most elaborate mechanism available.

Do Not Forget the Mattress

The lift system is designed to work with the complete bed setup, including the mattress. Mattress characteristics can affect the way a lift mechanism performs, so always follow the product's stated specifications.

What to Check Before Buying

  • Check how the ottoman bed opens.
  • Consider the furniture surrounding the bed.
  • Think about the size of the items you will store.
  • Consider how frequently you need access.
  • Check the available bedroom clearance.
  • Review the product's lift and mattress specifications.
  • Choose the arrangement that suits your actual room layout.
